Getting 404 errors? You need to run a local server. Open your terminal in this folder and run one of these:

python3 -m http.server 8080 npx serve . live-server .

Then open http://localhost:8080 in your browser.

Or upload to Netlify / Vercel by dragging the folder — no server needed once hosted.
