Survey Research

The system is broken for newcomers. Every time.

We surveyed real movers and identified 8 universal pain points — regardless of where they were going.

01
No guidance or clear process
  • No step-by-step onboarding sequence exists
  • Users don't know the correct order of tasks
  • No "day one" roadmap anywhere
Root cause — everything else stems from this.
02
Hidden circular dependencies
  • Bank needs phone, phone needs address, address needs bank
  • Requirements not transparent upfront
  • Prerequisites discovered too late
The system isn't complex — it's structurally broken for newcomers.
03
Fragmentation across platforms
  • No centralized place to complete tasks
  • Housing, banking, utilities in separate ecosystems
The experience is disjointed instead of unified.
04
Zero personalization
  • Generic filters miss lifestyle priorities
  • No mosque, gym, or school proximity weighting
You don't need more options — you need better filtering.
05
Information gaps & word of mouth
  • Critical solutions never officially communicated
  • Repeatable hacks like Monzo/Wise never productized
Valuable knowledge exists but is nowhere systematized.
06
Barriers for international users
  • Foreign income not accepted in housing markets
  • No local credit history = competitive disadvantage
Systems are biased toward locally established users.
07
High friction in essential setup
  • Banking, phone, utilities each require multiple steps
  • Utility setup lacks global standardization
Basic life setup feels harder than it should be.
08
Decision overload
  • Too many options, no clear best choice
  • Overwhelm leads to paralysis and bad decisions
Users need clarity, not more options.
